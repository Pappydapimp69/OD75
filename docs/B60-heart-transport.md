# B60 — Heart transport and audible upgrades

Prism purchases currently advance the shop after one spend. Heart Sense alternates large range jumps with flight multipliers. Pip credits each heart remotely, so trips and Heart Relay can repeat too quickly. Mix upgrades add quiet, overlapping voices, and their level detail skips bosses.

Keep the emotional shop open for repeated purchases; Continue advances explicitly. Heart Sense gives +8px per level through level 10, +2px thereafter (200px cap), and +2 carry capacity per level. Swift alone sets unburdened flight speed. Pip starts at 10 capacity; hearts weigh 3. Speed falls linearly to 50% at full capacity, capped there for the last heart that crosses capacity. Pip gathers around his own position, carries a visible loose cluster, and returns when full or no nearby hearts remain. Cargo does not expire. Meeting Pip within 30px delivers it. Stage-clear reunion banks carried hearts before the shop, without a combat proc; reset clears cargo.

Heart Relay requires a real delivery, lasts 0.5s + 0.1s per additional level, and has an 8s trigger cooldown. Preserve its existing attack strength, the bond meter, Rally/Cover/Setup, Ascension limits, currencies, hold-to-buy controls and Sound Lab pairings.

Replace overlapping mix additions with three recurring signatures: warm heartbeat/harmony, bright bell counter-melody, and syncopated bass/bubble texture. Every level adds arrangement detail in waves and bosses. Give each mix a short repeatable audition and automatically audition purchases, respecting mute. Keep music bounded and combat cues available.

Acceptance: repeat purchases with exact wallet deductions and no overspend; cargo deferred until delivery, exact accounting, overload limit, lifetime and stage/reset handling; slower post-10 growth, no Sense flight multiplier; weight/Swift interaction; Relay duration/cooldown and pause; cargo survives Rally and Ascension; each mix/level schedules distinctive music in waves and bosses, preview is free and mute-safe; all B59 regressions; desktop/phone UI and keyboard/pointer/gamepad routes; exact deployed bundle.

Playtest: does meeting a burdened Pip halfway feel useful, and are the three mix signatures recognizable under combat?
