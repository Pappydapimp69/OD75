# B67: Safeguarded emergency cargo

Supportive can require about 15 seconds to recover from one shield to two, while its dropped cargo previously expired after 10 seconds. Mark emergency-dropped hearts and suspend their lifetime only while the Supportive emergency remains active. Once shields reach two, each heart resumes its normal remaining lifetime and Pip may collect it. The combat tip shows the live safeguarded cargo count. Preserve positions, weight, currency, difficulty accounting, ordinary heart expiry, pause, and stage cleanup.

Validate a return lasting longer than 10 seconds, recovery-time countdown, ordinary-heart expiry, pause, repeat emergencies, collection, and browser copy. Playtest question: does guaranteed survival preserve the consequence of dropping cargo while avoiding an impossible recovery window?
